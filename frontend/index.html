How predictions work

Each prediction is an average over everything that could happen, weighted by how likely it is, not a best case. It combines the chance a player is fit and starts (from his club's depth chart and FPL's injury news) and plays 60 minutes; his expected goals and assists, adjusted for the opponent and for his finishing record; penalties, if he's his club's taker; the chance of a clean sheet; defensive contributions, saves, bonus and cards.

This season's numbers are blended steadily with previous seasons: the more a player plays this season, the more this season counts. Team strengths come from this season's expected goals. Tested on every gameweek of 2024/25 and 2025/26, these predictions pick better players than FPL's own expected points, which are just recent form.
